# TrueSherpa - Your AI-Powered Life Coach

TrueSherpa is a mobile app designed to help users take control of their lives with the power of AI. By integrating intelligent guidance, habit tracking, and personalized recommendations, TrueSherpa empowers users to become more organized, disciplined, and focused on their physical and mental well-being.

Whether you're looking to improve productivity, mindfulness, fitness, or mental resilience, TrueSherpa provides a structured approach to self-improvement—tailored to your unique needs.

## Features in Progress
- AI-driven habit coaching and goal-setting
- Personalized daily routines and productivity tracking
- Mental wellness insights and guided mindfulness exercises
- Fitness and lifestyle recommendations based on user data
